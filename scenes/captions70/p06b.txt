The stupidest* thing you can do to yourself
is to have high expectations* from other people.*
Why is your mood* dependent on whether
somebody else showed up* for you?
People will disappoint* you.
That's human nature.*
And sometimes, even the person
you would do anything* for, will not do the same* for you.
Guess what?
It's fine.!
Learn to keep your expectations
from people low!
and your expectations from yourself
high!
If something needs to get done,
you'll figure it out.*
If life gets difficult,*
you'll handle it.*
If somebody leaves,*
you'll keep moving.*
If nobody believes in the thing you're building,
you'll believe enough* for all of them.*
You need to stop giving everyone
the power* to mess with your head.
Expect less* from the world
and demand more! from yourself.
because then other people
can't mess with your peace,*
and you never stop
pushing yourself forward.*
